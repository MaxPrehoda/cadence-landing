v0.13.1

November 20, 2026 Release notes Download

Keep awake now stays on until you turn it off, the timer runs out, or your agents are really done.

Changed

  • Keep awake holds. Set it from your iPhone or the toolbar and it no longer drops back to off on its own.
    • Survives restarts. The choice is saved and comes back when Cadence starts again, after an update, a crash or a relaunch. A timer comes back with the time it had left.
    • Two layers. Cadence holds macOS's own no-sleep assertions itself, as well as running caffeinate, and checks every 15 seconds. Anything that stopped holding is started again instead of quietly turning off.
    • Until agents finish waits for real. An agent waiting on you counts as busy, since you'll answer it from your phone. Cadence turns off only after 10 minutes with no agent working or waiting, not one. When it can't tell, it stays awake.
    • Says why. When it turns off on its own, the iPhone card and the menu say so: the timer ran out, or no agent had worked or waited on you for 10 minutes.
  • On iPhone (Cadence 0.9.1 on TestFlight): changing Keep awake retries through a dropped connection and reads the Mac's answer back, so the card always shows what the Mac is really doing.

v0.12.0

November 20, 2026 Release notes Download

Cadence Drive: one folder every agent, computer and phone shares, sealed end to end. And every window moves better.

New

  • Cadence Drive (Cadence Pro). Turn it on in Settings → Agents → Cadence Drive, or from the new Drive tab in the panel. ~/Cadence Drive is then the same on every computer you've paired, and a second computer that turns it on joins the same drive. It's an ordinary folder, so Finder, editors and agents use it directly.
    • Sealed end to end. Files are encrypted with keys only your devices hold. The relay stores them but can't read their contents or their names.
    • Who wrote what, live. Cadence credits every change to the agent that made it. The toolbar's Drive mark spins a ring in each writing agent's colour; the panel shows files being written right now, with the text arriving.
    • Edits merge on their own. Edits to different lines combine; CSV and TSV files merge row by row. When two agents change the same lines, one card comes to your Inbox with both sides and Claude's combined version first. Keep that, either side, or both as separate files, with 2 seconds to undo.
    • Every line knows who wrote it. Open a file to see each part edged in its writer's colour, and drag the history bar to replay it. The last 200 versions of every file can be restored.
    • A real file browser in the panel: folders, list or grid, columns for who changed it, when and size, search (words or a regular expression), and a right-click menu to open, show in Finder, copy the path, rename, move, duplicate, see versions, hand to an agent or move to the Trash. Drag files in.
    • Trash. Deleted files, deleted here or on another computer, wait 30 days.
    • Briefs. When an agent finishes, its recap is saved in briefs/, so the next agent on any computer picks up where it left off.
    • Hand files to agents from the file view, the right-click menu, or by voice: double-tap ⌥ and say "Give the checkout agent the API notes".
    • Agents get Drive tools: drive_list, drive_read, drive_write and drive_search.
    • On iPhone (TestFlight beta): a Drive tab, collision cards in the Inbox, and "Save to Cadence Drive" in the share sheet, with an optional note to an agent.
  • Settings → Agents → Cadence Drive: which computers have it, a switch to keep Drive off the toolbar, Briefs on or off, and turning it off on one computer.

Changed

  • Motion, everywhere. A pass over every window on the Mac and the iPhone: buttons answer a press, menus and popovers grow from what opened them, lists move instead of jumping, and views slide with a sense of place. Everything is quicker on the way out than on the way in, nothing animates on keyboard shortcuts, and Reduce Motion is respected throughout.

v0.11.1

November 19, 2026 Release notes Download

Dictation keeps working after your Mac sleeps.

Fixed

  • Dictation after sleep. Each time the microphone was reopened (after your Mac slept, or when you switched mics), the old audio stream kept running alongside the new one, so the recorder heard the same sound more than once and dictation came out garbled or empty until you restarted Cadence. v0.11.0

November 18, 2026 Release notes Download

Your Macs and PCs can now see each other's agents, and Cadence can show you one question at a time.

New

  • Pair your computers. In Settings → Agents → Other machines, one computer shows a 12-character code and the other types it. Both need Cadence Pro. From then on each one shows the other's agents on the toolbar, in the Inbox and in the agents list, under that computer's name.
    • Read, reply and answer from either one. Open an agent on the other computer to read what it has been doing, send it a message, or answer the question it is waiting on.
    • It travels the same sealed route your iPhone uses, so it works from anywhere and the relay can't read it.
    • Your agents can see paired computers too, through the tools added in 0.10.0. Anything an agent does on another computer still asks you first.
  • One question at a time (off by default; the switch is in the Judgment view). With it on, the Inbox and your iPhone show only the question whose answer is worth the most right now, with a quiet "N more waiting" for the rest. How big the decision is, how sure Cadence already is of your answer, and how long it has waited decide the order.
  • Cadence for Windows is built from the same code as the Mac app from this release on. The Windows installer is on the release below and at cadencevoice.ai/windows. It is unsigned for now, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.

Changed

  • Agents on other machines use herdr's own readiness and finished-turn signals where it provides them.
  • The agents' tools for seeing and messaging agents on other machines now work on Windows as well.
  • The pairing code is short enough to type: 12 letters and digits, good for ten minutes, working once.

Fixed

  • The toolbar's panel opens beside the bar as drawn, and its hover list opens when only outside agents or closed sets are there.
  • A rejected plan window is no longer treated as a failure while extra usage carries the request.
  • Usage counts are collected correctly on Windows for both Claude Code and Codex.

v0.10.0

November 17, 2026 Release notes Download

Steadier dictation and agents, agents that can see your other machines, and the numbers behind a usage screen on your iPhone.

New

  • Agents can see and message agents on your other machines. For the machines you've connected under Settings → Agents → Other machines, your agents and the Director can now list every agent, read what it has been doing, and send it a message.
    • Looking is allowed; acting asks you first. A message to another machine, or starting an agent there, shows the whole message on an approval card, and each machine has its own "let my agents act here" setting.
    • Text read from another machine is treated as information, never as instructions.
  • Usage for the iPhone. Cadence now keeps a running count of the tokens your agents use, by day, model, project and agent, across Claude Code, Codex and local models, and serves it to Cadence for iPhone 0.7.0. Only counts leave your Mac, never the text of a conversation.

Changed

  • Pro. A purchase started from Cadence is picked up even if the app restarts before you finish paying. Settings → Pro shows when a cancelled plan ends, and a plan switched between monthly and yearly now shows correctly.
  • Cloud. Work an agent did in the cloud always comes home to your Mac, including after Pro has lapsed or the iPhone remote was switched off.
  • With Accessibility switched off, dictated words now stay on the clipboard with a message telling you so, where they used to be lost. What you had copied before is replaced.
  • The main window hides when closed and reopens from the Dock.

Fixed

  • Dictation
    • Short answers such as "No." could be thrown away as an echo of the prompt.
    • A correction rule could rewrite the inside of another word ("today" becoming "tooday"), and an empty rule could hang dictation.
    • A stuck polish model no longer freezes dictation: the words are typed as spoken.
    • A hotkey press or release that arrived during a paste could be missed, leaving the key "stuck".
    • A microphone that goes quiet after sleep or a device switch is reopened, and a muted microphone now says "Mic is silent".
    • Holding the key while the speech model is still loading no longer types the words seconds later into whatever has focus.
  • Agents and approvals
    • Approvals could stop arriving after Cadence had been running for more than three days.
    • A question from an agent that then crashed or was stopped no longer stays in the Inbox for ever.
    • Agents shown as busy or idle when they were not.
    • Browser find and click no longer lets crafted text run script in the page.
  • Pro and billing
    • A problem reaching the payment provider can no longer switch a paying user back to Free.
    • Away mode stays on through a brief licence check at launch.
  • Stability
    • A damaged settings file is recovered field by field and a copy is kept, so settings are never silently reset.
    • A model download that stalls no longer hangs.
  • Many smaller fixes across the windows: errors that were swallowed are now shown, and keyboard and screen-reader use is better in Settings and the toolbar.

v0.9.0

Continuous Talk: keep talking, and Cadence sends each request when you've finished it.

New

  • Continuous Talk. Triple-tap your Talk key. (Double-tap still opens ordinary Talk, and hold still dictates.) The microphone stays on, and Cadence listens for the moment a request is complete.
    • A finished request gets a five-second preview with Keep talking and Send now. Start speaking again and the send is cancelled, so an afterthought ("…but not on main") stays part of the same request.

    • The Director hands its request to Codex on the same conversation. A turn that had already started changing something is never replayed automatically.
    • Cadence never buys credits or uses API keys. It only uses the accounts you're already signed in to.

v0.7.1

October 19, 2026 Release notes Download

Cadence now aims higher when it picks work for your agents: bigger goals, its own ideas for long jobs toward them, and agents that keep going for hours until the job is done.

Changed

  • Goals aim higher. Each project's goal is now the outcome you could reach in about three months if your agents did most of the building, with a bar you can check ("25 people use it every day by January"). Goals Cadence drafted before redraft once on their own; goals you wrote stay as you wrote them.
  • Cadence's own ideas. Besides what you've said and tracked, Cadence reads each project's repo (what's missing, thin or unfinished) and proposes long jobs toward your goal, each a few hours of agent work, quoting the gap it rests on. They're marked Cadence's idea with about how many hours they take, start only on your yes, and turning one down never counts against Cadence's picks.
  • Agents keep going. A milestone agent ends each reply with where it stands. While there's more to do, Cadence tells it to keep going; when it needs you, its question waits in your Inbox; when it's done, its work is checked against your rules and waits for your tap. It stops when it's stuck (three turns without a new commit) or after ten hours, and its in-between turns don't fill your Inbox.
  • Run more at once. The day plan shows up to eight jobs and lets you choose how many run at once (three, unless you change it, up to six).

Cadence now learns how you decide and what you'd work on next, so your agents keep going while you're away. It guesses your answers before you give them, earns the right to give them itself, proposes the next task in each project with where the idea came from, runs it in its own worktree, and has the finished work waiting for your tap when you're back.

The scales button in the panel opens what Cadence has learned: the rules you've taught Claude in your memory notes and your own CLAUDE.md, and the corrections you make as you answer.   • Step away. The moon button, or telling Talk "I'm stepping away", shows the day plan: what Cadence will start, what it answers for you, and how quiet your phone stays. When you're back, the briefing leads with what got done.
  • Take it with you. Export your judgment as an AGENTS.md with your rules and goals, or import a teammate's rules; each one waits for your yes.

Changed

  • Your phone stays quiet while you're away: one push only when every agent is waiting on you, at most three a day.

v0.5.5

October 16, 2026 Release notes Download

Dictation appears as you speak, updates install themselves, and Claude Code in your other terminals shows up on the toolbar.

New

  • Dictation types as you speak. Hold ⌥ and your words start appearing in the field about a second in, a few at a time. When you let go, Cadence swaps the rough text for the polished version in place, usually in well under a second. It works in native apps, browsers, Electron apps like Slack and VS Code, and terminals. Password fields never get text while you speak, and your clipboard is put back afterwards. Turn it off in Settings → Injection → Type as you speak.
  • Updates install themselves. Cadence checks 5 seconds after launch and then every 6 hours. When an update is out, the toolbar, the ⋯ menu and the Inbox say so, and Install and relaunch downloads it, checks its checksum, signature and notarization, swaps it in and reopens Cadence. This works from 0.5.5 on, so this update still installs the old way.
  • Claude Code from your other terminals. Claude Code sessions running in Terminal or iTerm show on the toolbar after a thin divider: blue while working, amber when asking you something, green when done. Click one to jump to that exact tab. The Inbox shows the question with Answer in Terminal, and finished sessions offer Move to Cadence.
  • Keep your Mac awake. In the toolbar's ⋯ menu: on, for 1 or 4 hours, or until your agents finish. A MacBook with its lid closed still sleeps unless it is plugged in with an external display.
  • Ready for the iPhone app. The Mac side of dictating replies, starting new agents, Talk, Keep awake, Inbox details and notifications from your phone. The iPhone app is in testing.

Changed

  • Faster polish. The on-device polish model now drafts ahead, about 40% faster on full sentences, with the same output.
