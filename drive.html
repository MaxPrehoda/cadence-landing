03 / Edits merge on their own

Two agents, one file. Both edits stay.

Edits to different lines of a file combine automatically. CSV and TSV files merge row by row, so two agents adding rows to one table both get their rows in.

  • 3.1
    Same lines? You decide

    When two agents change the same lines, a card arrives in the Inbox on your Mac and your iPhone. It shows both sides, with a combined version by Claude first.

  • 3.2
    Four ways out

    Keep the combined version, keep either side, or keep both as separate files. You get two seconds to undo, and both agents get the result.

Limits

How it stays private

Sealed on your devices. The relay just holds it.

Cadence's relay stores your files for your other devices. It only ever has sealed copies, and the keys to open them never leave your devices.

1 · On your device

Sealed where it's written

Before a file leaves your computer or phone, Cadence encrypts its contents and its name with AES-256-GCM.

2 · At the relay

Stored, not read

The relay stores the sealed copy for your other devices. It has no key, so it can't open the file or see what it's called.

3 · On your other devices

Opened only by you

The keys are held only by your devices. Each one opens what the relay hands it and writes the file into ~/Cadence Drive.

Questions

What people ask about Cadence Drive

Is it a normal folder?

Yes. It's ~/Cadence Drive in your home folder. Finder, your editor, your terminal and every agent open it like any other folder. Cadence keeps it the same on each computer you've paired and turned Drive on.

Can Cadence read my files?

No. Each file is sealed on your device with AES-256-GCM, using keys that only your devices hold. The relay stores the sealed copies, but it can't read what's in them or what they're called.

What happens when two agents edit the same file?

Edits to different lines combine on their own, and CSV and TSV files merge row by row. When two agents change the same lines, a card arrives in the Inbox on your Mac and iPhone with both sides and a combined version by Claude. Keep the combined version, either side, or both as separate files. There's a 2-second undo, and both agents get the result.

Can I get an old version back?

Yes. Open the file and use the history bar to replay it and restore any of its last 200 versions. Deleted files, deleted on this computer or another one, wait in the Trash for 30 days.

How big can files be?

Up to 512 MB each. Empty folders sync once a file is in them.
