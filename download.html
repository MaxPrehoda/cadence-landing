Download

Cadence is downloading.
Do these three things next.

The file goes to your Downloads folder. If nothing started, press the button.

↓ Download again Cadence for Mac · Apple Silicon · macOS 13 or later
  1. Drag to Applications

    1Drag Cadence to Applications

    Open the Cadence disk image in your Downloads folder and drag Cadence into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS asks whether to open an app from the internet. Click Open. Cadence is signed and notarized by Apple.

  2. MicrophoneAccessibilityInput Monitoring

    2Allow three permissions

    Cadence asks for each one the first time it opens. Dictation doesn't work without them.

    Microphone
    To hear you while you hold the key. Your audio never leaves your Mac.
    Accessibility
    To type your words into the app you're in.
    Input Monitoring
    To notice the hotkey in any app.
  3. hold ⌥ and talk double-tap ⌥ for your agents

    3Hold ⌥ and talk

    Setup downloads the speech model once, about 630 MB. After that, hold ⌥ in any app, talk, and the text is typed where your cursor is. Double-tap ⌥ to give a task to your agents.
