Pricing

Free while you're watching. Pro when you're not.

At your Mac, Cadence costs nothing and has no agent limit. Pro is for when you leave: Cadence answers the questions it already knows your answer to, keeps your agents working for hours, and puts the Inbox on your iPhone.

Free

At your desk

$0

  • Dictation in any app, running on your Mac
  • Unlimited agents in one Inbox: Claude Code, Codex and eight more
  • Talk: say a task and it goes to the right agent. Arrange your windows by voice too
  • It learns how you answer: Cadence guesses before you do, shows whether it matched, and ⏎ takes its pick
Download for Mac

macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon. No account.

Pro

When you're not there

  • It answers for you: once Cadence has matched you often enough on a kind of question, it answers those itself. esc stops it, and one wrong answer sends it back to asking. It never answers anything that leaves your Mac or can't be undone
  • Away mode: say "I'm stepping away". Cadence starts the day's plan and keeps your agents working for hours. Your phone buzzes three times a day at most
  • Its own long jobs: Cadence reads your repos and proposes hours of work toward your goals. One key starts a job on its own branch. Nothing is pushed until you say ship it
  • iPhone: answer agents from your phone, at home or anywhere, end-to-end encrypted

Checkout by Stripe. You get a license key; Cadence turns Pro on by itself if you bought from the app.

Do I need an account?

No. Pro is a license key. Buy from Cadence's Settings → Pro and it turns on by itself; bought here, paste the key there.

What do my agents cost?

Agents use your own Claude Code plan, or whichever provider each CLI is set up with. Cadence Pro doesn't change that.

What happens if I stop paying?

Cadence goes back to Free: it keeps learning and suggesting, and asks you every time. What it learned stays on your Mac, so Pro picks up where it left off.

How do I cancel or get an invoice?

Settings → Pro → Manage billing opens Stripe's billing portal, where you can cancel, change your card or plan and download invoices. No key to hand? Sign in to billing with the email you bought with.

I lost my license key

Email support@cadencevoice.ai from the address you bought with and we'll send it to you.
