Pricing

Free while you're watching. Pro when you're not.

Everything you do at your desk is free, with every agent you run. Pro is Cadence carrying the work while you're away from it.

Checkout by Stripe. You get a license key; Cadence turns Pro on by itself if you bought from the app.

Do I need an account?

No. Pro is a license key. Buy from Cadence's Settings → Pro and it turns on by itself; bought here, paste the key there.

What do my agents cost?

Agents use your own Claude Code plan, or whichever provider each CLI is set up with. Cadence Pro doesn't change that.

What happens if I stop paying?

Cadence goes back to Free: it keeps learning and suggesting, and asks you every time. What it learned stays on your Mac, so Pro picks up where it left off.

How do I cancel or get an invoice?

Settings → Pro → Manage billing opens Stripe's billing portal. Every invoice shows your license key. Lost it? Email support@cadencevoice.ai.
