What Cadence does

It types what you say,
and it shows you which agent is stuck.

Hold ⌥ and talk: the words are typed into whatever app you're in. Double-tap ⌥ and say what you want built: Cadence gives it to the agent already working on that, or starts a new one. When an agent stops to ask you something, you see it right away, not an hour later in a terminal tab you forgot about.

1.0 / Dictation

Hold ⌥ and talk
in any app

Hold the key, talk, let go. The text is typed where your cursor is: Slack, Mail, your editor, a terminal. Speech recognition runs on your Mac, so your audio never leaves it.

  • 1.1
    Text a third of a second after you let go

    NVIDIA's Parakeet model transcribes while you're still talking, so only the last few words are left when you release. A second pass gets names right.

  • 1.2
    It knows your words

    Add project names, people and jargon once, and set a correction for any word it keeps getting wrong.

  • 1.3
    Text Polish

    A small model on your Mac takes out the ums, fixes punctuation, and writes casual in Slack and formal in email.

2.0 / Talk and Director

Double-tap ⌥ and say the task.
Cadence picks the agent.

You don't choose an agent or a folder. Cadence reads what every agent is working on, the window in front of you and a screenshot of your screen, then sends your request where it belongs. Typing works too.

  • 2.1
    Follow-ups reach the same agent

    "Also make it blue" goes to whoever got your last request. New work starts a new agent in the right folder. Either way you get a receipt, like "Sent to claude-2, the landing page thread".

  • 2.2
    The Director

    One agent that can read every other agent. Ask it "what's left before I can ship?" or "is anything stuck?". Tell it to start, message or stop agents and it does.

  • 2.3
    Titles, not process IDs

    Every agent gets a short title like "settings page dark mode", so the list reads like a to-do list.

  • 2.4
    Sets

    Close a whole project's terminal windows with one click. The agents keep running, and one chip turns amber if any of them needs you.

3.0 / Toolbar and Inbox

One dot per agent.
Amber means it's waiting on you.

Blue is working, amber is waiting on you, green is done. Open the Inbox and every waiting agent is there as a card with numbered answers.

  • 3.1
    Hover to read, click to open

    Hover the dots to see every agent's title. Click one to go straight to that agent.

  • 3.2
    Answer with a number

    Questions, plan approvals and Allow/Deny prompts become numbered options. Press 1 to 9, or start typing a reply.

  • 3.3
    25 words, not 400 lines

    When an agent finishes you get a recap of at most 25 words, plus replies you can send with one key when there's an obvious next step.

  • 3.4
    No mouse needed

    J K to move, E to dismiss, V to talk, S to attach a screenshot. You get two seconds to undo any answer.

New in 0.12 / Cadence Drive

One folder on every computer.
Every agent can write to it.

Cadence Drive is a real folder, ~/Cadence Drive, kept the same on every computer you've paired with Cadence and on your iPhone. Finder, your editor and every agent use it like any other folder. It comes with Cadence Pro.

  • 5.1
    Who wrote what, live

    A ring on the toolbar shows which agent is writing. Open a file and a coloured edge shows who wrote each part.

  • 5.2
    Edits merge on their own

    Changes to different lines combine. When two agents change the same lines, the Inbox shows both sides with Claude's combined version first.

  • 5.3
    Sealed end to end

    AES-256-GCM, with keys only your devices hold. The relay stores your files but can't read them or their names.

  • 5.4
    Nothing gets lost

    Restore any of a file's last 200 versions. Deleted files wait in the Trash for 30 days.

Questions

What people ask
before they install

Is Cadence another coding agent?

No. Cadence doesn't write code. It runs on top of the agents you already use, like Claude Code and Codex: it shows you what each one is waiting for, sends your answer back, and gives new requests to the right one.

Does Cadence act on my agents by itself?

It never answers for you. Questions, plan approvals and Allow/Deny prompts wait in your Inbox until you answer, and every answer has a two-second take-back. Which tools an agent may use without asking is up to you, the same as in the CLI.

The Director only starts, messages or closes agents when you ask it to.

Do my agents have to run in herdr?

Not Claude Code: Cadence runs it directly, with real Allow/Deny, cost tracking and worktree diffs. Every other CLI (Codex, OpenCode, Gemini, Amp, Cursor, Copilot, Grok, Pi, Omp) runs in herdr, an open-source terminal workspace, and opens in a Cadence terminal window. Their questions land in the same Inbox.

What does it cost?

Nothing at your desk. Dictation, every agent, the Inbox, Talk, and Cadence learning how you decide are free, with no account. Cadence Pro is for when you're not there: answering for you, away mode, long runs, its own ideas, the iPhone app and Cadence Drive. $20 a month, $200 a year, or $249 once for the first 100.

Your agents keep using the plans you already pay for: your Claude Code login, or whichever provider each CLI is set up with.

Can I use it from my phone?

Yes, in beta, as part of Cadence Pro. The iPhone app is in a private TestFlight beta: it pairs with your Mac over a QR code, traffic between them is end-to-end encrypted, and you answer your agents from Home, a widget or Siri. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email when it opens up. More in Claude Code on your iPhone.

What does Cadence send, and where does it go?

Speech recognition and Text Polish run on your Mac, and audio is never uploaded. Agents, routing, titles and recaps use your own Claude Code login; other agents talk to their own providers, as they already do. Cloud Polish and the Intent Engine only run if you add your own Anthropic API key. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work offline?

Dictation and Text Polish do, once their models are downloaded. Agents, Talk routing and the Director need a connection, because they talk to Claude or your other agents' providers.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 13 or later. For agents, the claude CLI installed and signed in, plus herdr if you want agents other than Claude Code. Dictation works without either.

On Windows? There is an early preview for Windows 11. It has not been tested on Windows yet. Cadence for Windows says what to expect.
