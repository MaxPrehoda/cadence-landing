What Cadence does

Keep writing the code.
Let Cadence keep the agents moving.

Five terminals, three of them waiting on a yes, and you find out ten minutes later when you happen to tab back. Cadence watches every agent for you, brings you only what needs a decision, and sends your answer back to the right place.

1.0 / Toolbar and Inbox

See every agent
at a glance

A thin toolbar on the edge of your screen shows one dot per agent. Blue is working, amber needs you, green is done. Open the Inbox and answer in a keystroke.

  • 1.1
    Status at a glance

    Hover the agent pill to peek at every agent's title. Click any agent to jump straight to it.

  • 1.2
    Numbered answers

    Questions, plan approvals and Allow/Deny prompts become numbered options. Press 1 to 9, or start typing a reply.

  • 1.3
    Recaps of finished work

    When an agent finishes, you get a short recap and suggested next steps instead of a wall of output.

  • 1.4
    Keyboard first

    J K to move, E to dismiss, V to talk, S to attach a screenshot. Every answer has a two-second take-back.

2.0 / Talk and Director

Say it once.
It finds the right agent.

Double-tap ⌥ and say what you want, or type it. Cadence knows what each agent is working on, which app you're looking at and which repos you have.

  • 2.1
    Routing

    Your request goes to the agent already on that work, or Cadence starts a new one in the right folder. It tells you where it went.

  • 2.2
    The Director

    An always-on agent that can see every other agent, read their work, message them, start new ones and close finished ones. Ask "what's left before I can ship?"

  • 2.3
    Titles, not process IDs

    Every agent gets a short plain-English title, so your list reads like a to-do list.

  • 2.4
    Sets

    Put a whole project's agents away as a set. Their windows close, they keep running, and one chip turns amber if anything needs you.

3.0 / Dictation

Hold ⌥ and talk
in any app

The same key types for you everywhere: Slack, Mail, your editor, a terminal. Speech recognition runs on your Mac, so your audio never leaves it.

  • 3.1
    On-device speech

    NVIDIA's Parakeet model transcribes while you hold the key, with a second pass to get names right.

  • 3.2
    Your vocabulary

    Teach it project names, people and jargon, and set auto-corrections for the words it keeps missing.

  • 3.3
    Text Polish

    A small local model cleans up filler words and punctuation, and adjusts tone per app: casual in Slack, formal in email.

Questions

What people ask
before they install

Is Cadence another coding agent?

No. It sits on top of the agents you already run, like Claude Code and Codex, and keeps them moving: it shows you what they need, sends your answers back, and routes new requests to the right one.

Does Cadence act on my agents by itself?

It never answers for you. Questions, plan approvals and Allow/Deny prompts wait in your Inbox until you answer, and every answer has a two-second take-back. Which tools an agent may use without asking is up to you, the same as in the CLI.

The Director only starts, messages or closes agents when you ask it to.

Do my agents have to run in herdr?

Not Claude Code: Cadence runs it directly, with real Allow/Deny, cost tracking and worktree diffs. Every other CLI (Codex, OpenCode, Gemini, Amp, Cursor, Copilot, Grok, Pi, Omp) runs in herdr, an open-source terminal workspace, and opens in a Cadence terminal window. Their questions land in the same Inbox.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Cadence is free, with no account and no subscription. Your agents keep using the plans you already pay for: your Claude Code login, or whichever provider each CLI is set up with.

Can I use it from my phone?

Soon. The iPhone app pairs with your Mac over a QR code, and traffic between them is end-to-end encrypted. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email when it's ready.

What does Cadence send, and where does it go?

Speech recognition and Text Polish run on your Mac, and audio is never uploaded. Agents, routing, titles and recaps use your own Claude Code login; other agents talk to their own providers, as they already do. Cloud Polish and the Intent Engine only run if you add your own Anthropic API key. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work offline?

Dictation and Text Polish do, once their models are downloaded. Agents, Talk routing and the Director need a connection, because they talk to Claude or your other agents' providers.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 13 or later. For agents, the claude CLI installed and signed in, plus herdr if you want agents other than Claude Code. Dictation works without either.
